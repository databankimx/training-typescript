# Ch04 · Numbers

Numbers are the values most programs are really about. Prices, scores, and counts all need careful handling, and the way computers store decimals has a few habits that will surprise you. This chapter covers the common ones, so the surprises at least arrive expected.

## What you'll learn

1. What a number is, and how to check whether a value is a whole number.
2. How the arithmetic operators work, and the order in which they're applied.
3. How to round a number, and how to show it with a fixed number of decimal places.
4. Why some decimal sums are slightly wrong, and how to avoid the problem with money.
5. How numbers can be written in binary and hexadecimal.
6. How to turn text into a number, and what happens when the text isn't a number.
7. The `Math` library, which does the common calculations for you.

The chapter ends with a summary, and two homework exercises.
