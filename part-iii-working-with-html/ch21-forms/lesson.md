# Ch21 - Forms

Forms are how a learner gives a program information: an order, a sign-up, a search. The browser does a lot of the work for you, such as sending the form when the learner presses Submit, and checking that a required field has a value. The script's job is to read what was entered, decide whether it's acceptable, and show the learner the result. Each of those jobs has a typed API, and this chapter covers them in turn.

These lessons are page steps, as in the last chapter. Press **Run** to open the page in its own window, and use the **File** drop-down to edit the page's HTML and CSS as well as its script.

## What you'll learn

1. How to read a submitted form, and handle its submit event without the page reloading.
2. How to check a field's validity, and give the learner a clear message.
3. How to read a number input as a number, and format it for display.
4. How to show and hide fields that depend on another choice.

The chapter ends with a summary, and two homework exercises.
